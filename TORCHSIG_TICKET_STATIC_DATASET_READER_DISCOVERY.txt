Title: StaticTorchSigDataset should discover the reader recorded by DatasetCreator

Summary

DatasetCreator in TorchSig 2.2 can infer the reader paired with a known writer
and records that reader in writer_info.yaml. StaticTorchSigDataset nevertheless
defaults unconditionally to HDF5Reader unless the caller explicitly supplies
file_handler_class.

This means a dataset successfully generated with HomogeneousHDF5Writer cannot
be opened with the otherwise natural call:

    StaticTorchSigDataset(root=dataset_root)

The caller must know and repeat the storage choice:

    StaticTorchSigDataset(
        root=dataset_root,
        file_handler_class=HomogeneousHDF5Reader,
    )

Impact

- Writer/reader selection must be threaded through downstream libraries even
  though DatasetCreator already persisted the selected reader.
- Omitting the explicit reader attempts to interpret homogeneous storage using
  the legacy reader and produces a loading error.
- Storage metadata can become inconsistent with the reader selected by a
  caller.
- Adding another backend requires every consumer to add matching selection
  logic.

Proposed behavior

When file_handler_class is omitted, StaticTorchSigDataset should read
writer_info.yaml and resolve the recorded file_reader or
file_reader_qualified value. It should retain HDF5Reader as the fallback for
legacy datasets whose metadata does not contain a reader.

An explicitly supplied file_handler_class should continue to take precedence.
If the explicit reader conflicts with a known recorded reader, TorchSig should
either raise a clear compatibility error or document that the override is
intentional.

Acceptance criteria

- A dataset created with HomogeneousHDF5Writer can be loaded by passing only
  its root to StaticTorchSigDataset.
- Legacy HDF5 datasets without reader metadata still use HDF5Reader.
- An explicit file_handler_class continues to work.
- Missing, malformed, or unimportable reader metadata raises a clear error or
  follows a documented safe fallback.
- Tests cover legacy HDF5, homogeneous HDF5, explicit override, and malformed
  writer metadata.

Workaround

Downstream callers must currently pass the matching reader explicitly. The
torchsig-models integration passes both file_handler and file_reader through
its dataset and training utilities.
