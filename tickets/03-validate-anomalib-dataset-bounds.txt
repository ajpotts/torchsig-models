Title: Validate Anomalib dataset sizes, splits, and index bounds

Criticality: High
Estimated effort: Low
Type: Bug

Description:
TorchSigAnomalibDataModule accepts a requested subset size without checking it
against the underlying dataset length. Split proportions are also not checked
for count, sign, range, or total. The resulting inclusive index bounds can be
negative, overlap incorrectly, or extend beyond the static dataset and cause an
IndexError during iteration.

Direct TorchSigAnomalibDataset construction also defaults index_bounds to
[0, 0], which exposes only the first sample. An omitted bound should normally
mean the complete dataset.

Evidence:
- torchsig_models/adapters/anomalib_utils.py, TorchSigAnomalibDataset.__init__,
  __len__, and __getitem__
- torchsig_models/adapters/anomalib_utils.py,
  TorchSigAnomalibDataModule.__init__ split calculations

Proposed resolution:
- Validate that size is positive and no greater than full_dataset_size.
- Require exactly three nonnegative split proportions whose sum is approximately
  one.
- Validate all explicit inclusive bounds against the dataset length.
- Replace mutable/default [0, 0] bounds with None and resolve None to the full
  dataset.

Acceptance criteria:
- Valid splits cover the requested subset exactly once with no overlap or gaps.
- Oversized, zero, or negative sizes raise a clear ValueError.
- Invalid split counts, negative values, and invalid totals raise ValueError.
- Direct construction without bounds exposes the full static dataset.
- Tests cover normal, zero-length-boundary, and invalid-input cases.
