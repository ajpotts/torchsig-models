Title: Benchmark input and feature downsampling across other model families

Criticality: Medium
Estimated effort: High
Type: Performance investigation

Description:
The EfficientNet-1D temporal-downsampling investigation should be complemented
by model-appropriate input reduction studies for other families. IQ XCiT can
reduce token sequence length, spectrogram classifiers and detectors can reduce
time/frequency resolution or patch count, and anomaly models may reduce image
resolution. These choices can improve throughput and memory use but may erase
short, narrowband, or small-object features.

Evidence:
- tickets/performance/06-benchmark-temporal-downsampling.txt defines the
  EfficientNet-1D quality and performance comparison
- torchsig_models/models/iq_models/xcit/xcit1d.py tokenizes long IQ inputs
- EfficientNet-2D, YOLO, and Anomalib consume image-like spectrogram inputs
  whose dimensions directly affect compute and activation memory

Proposed resolution:
For each model family, identify the supported reduction control: signal-domain
resampling or token stride for IQ XCiT; FFT, hop, image, patch, or feature-map
resolution for spectrogram models. Benchmark only semantically valid settings,
with suitable anti-aliasing and label/box transformations. Treat changes that
alter learned representations or checkpoint shapes as new configurations.

Acceptance criteria:
- At least the current setting and one reduced-compute setting are compared
  for every supported model family with an applicable control.
- Results include throughput, peak memory, parameter count or FLOPs, and
  task-appropriate validation/test metrics.
- Signal resampling documents anti-aliasing, and image resizing documents how
  annotations and small targets are preserved.
- Public controls validate their values and have output-shape tests.
- Checkpoint and configuration compatibility are documented.
- Models without a meaningful downsampling control are explicitly excluded
  with rationale rather than given an artificial common setting.
- No reduced-resolution setting becomes a default without a model-specific
  accuracy tradeoff assessment.
