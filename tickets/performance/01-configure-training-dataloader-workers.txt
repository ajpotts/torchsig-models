Title: Configure EfficientNet-1D training data loaders for GPU throughput

Criticality: High
Estimated effort: Medium
Type: Performance improvement

Description:
prepare_torchsig_datasets constructs the train, validation, and test loaders
without setting num_workers, pin_memory, or persistent_workers. The resulting
num_workers=0 default performs all static-dataset reads and collation in the
training process and can leave the GPU idle between batches.

Evidence:
- torchsig_models/utils/datasets.py, prepare_torchsig_datasets
- torchsig_models/utils/datasets.py, prepare_torchsig_inference_dataset already
  exposes num_workers and pin_memory
- torchsig_models/models/iq_models/efficientnet/efficientnet1d_train.py calls
  prepare_torchsig_datasets without loader-performance options

Proposed resolution:
Add configurable num_workers, pin_memory, and persistent_workers options to
prepare_torchsig_datasets and the EfficientNet-1D training entry point. Choose
safe defaults, enable persistent workers only when num_workers is greater than
zero, and preserve deterministic worker seeding. Consider exposing prefetch_factor
when profiling shows it is useful.

Acceptance criteria:
- Training, validation, and test loader worker counts are configurable.
- Pinning can be enabled for CUDA training and disabled for CPU-only runs.
- persistent_workers is never passed as true when num_workers is zero.
- Existing deterministic worker seeding behavior is preserved.
- Tests cover zero-worker and multi-worker loader configuration.
- A documented benchmark compares examples per second and GPU utilization
  before and after the change.
