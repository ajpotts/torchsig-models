Title: Enable mixed precision for EfficientNet-1D training

Criticality: High
Estimated effort: Low
Type: Performance improvement

Description:
The shared train_validate function supports Lightning precision modes, but
train_efficientnet_iq does not expose or forward that setting. EfficientNet-1D
therefore uses the shared 32-true default even on GPUs that can train faster
with bf16-mixed or 16-mixed precision.

Evidence:
- torchsig_models/utils/training.py, train_validate has precision="32-true"
- torchsig_models/models/iq_models/efficientnet/efficientnet1d_train.py does
  not pass precision to train_validate

Proposed resolution:
Expose precision through train_efficientnet_iq, its CLI, and training parameter
files, then forward it to train_validate. Document bf16-mixed as the preferred
option on supported hardware and 16-mixed as the fallback. Keep 32-true
available for CPU and numerical debugging.

Acceptance criteria:
- The requested precision reaches pytorch_lightning.Trainer.
- Existing callers that omit precision retain a documented compatible default.
- CPU-only execution remains supported.
- Tests verify forwarding of 32-true, 16-mixed, and bf16-mixed values.
- A representative GPU benchmark reports throughput, peak memory, and final
  validation accuracy relative to 32-true.
