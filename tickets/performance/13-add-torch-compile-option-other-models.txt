Title: Evaluate optional compilation for all model families

Criticality: Medium
Estimated effort: High
Type: Performance improvement

Description:
The optional torch.compile path proposed for EfficientNet-1D should be
evaluated for every PyTorch model family. Compilation gains and graph breaks
differ for transformer, two-dimensional convolution, detection, and anomaly
detection workloads, and framework-managed trainers may already compile or
wrap models in ways that constrain direct use of torch.compile.

Evidence:
- tickets/performance/05-add-torch-compile-option.txt defines the
  EfficientNet-1D compile controls and benchmark requirements
- EfficientNet-2D uses the shared Lightning training path
- IQ XCiT constructs its model and Lightning Trainer directly
- YOLO and Anomalib own portions of model construction and training through
  their respective frameworks

Proposed resolution:
Add a consistent opt-in compile setting to native EfficientNet-2D and IQ XCiT
training paths, including configurable compile mode and clear failures. Audit
YOLO and Anomalib for supported framework-native compilation controls rather
than bypassing their lifecycle. Record graph breaks and effective settings,
keep eager execution as the compatible fallback, and apply the same policy to
future model training entry points.

Acceptance criteria:
- Native model training can enable or disable compilation without changing
  model factories.
- Compile mode and effective compile status are recorded in logs or metadata.
- Unsupported framework/model combinations fail clearly or use an explicitly
  requested eager fallback; fallback is never silent.
- Tests verify option plumbing without performing a costly real compilation.
- Benchmarks report compile time, first-epoch time, steady-state epoch time,
  total run time, peak memory, and graph breaks for each model family.
- Compiled and eager outputs agree within model- and precision-appropriate
  tolerances, and checkpoint save/load remains compatible.
- Eager mode remains the default for each family until its own results justify
  a change.
