Title: Extend mixed-precision training to all model families

Criticality: High
Estimated effort: Medium
Type: Performance improvement

Description:
The configurable Lightning precision support proposed for EfficientNet-1D
should be applied consistently to every model training path. EfficientNet-2D
uses the shared train_validate helper but does not expose or forward its
precision option. IQ XCiT constructs a Lightning Trainer directly without a
precision setting. These paths therefore default to full precision even on
hardware where bf16-mixed or 16-mixed training may improve throughput and
reduce activation memory.

YOLO and Anomalib manage precision through their own training frameworks. Their
automatic-mixed-precision behavior and override mechanisms should be exposed
and recorded rather than assumed. Spectrogram XCiT and DETR should include the
same explicit precision policy when supported training entry points are added.

Evidence:
- tickets/performance/02-enable-mixed-precision-training.txt defines the
  EfficientNet-1D precision requirements
- torchsig_models/utils/training.py accepts a Lightning precision value and
  forwards it to pytorch_lightning.Trainer
- torchsig_models/models/spectrogram_models/efficientnet/efficientnet_train.py
  calls train_validate without forwarding precision
- torchsig_models/models/iq_models/xcit/xcit1d_train.py constructs
  pytorch_lightning.Trainer without specifying precision
- YOLO and Anomalib training are framework-managed and require separate
  validation of their AMP configuration and checkpoint behavior

Proposed resolution:
Expose precision through every native model training API, CLI, and applicable
configuration file. Forward the setting to Lightning or the corresponding
framework-native AMP control and record the effective precision in run and
checkpoint metadata. Document bf16-mixed as preferred on supported hardware,
16-mixed as a tested fallback, and 32-true for CPU execution, compatibility,
and numerical debugging.

Validate numerically sensitive model-specific operations under reduced
precision rather than assuming that one setting is safe for all architectures.
Add an explicit precision option as part of future spectrogram XCiT and DETR
training entry points.

Acceptance criteria:
- EfficientNet-2D and IQ XCiT forward the requested precision to Lightning.
- YOLO and Anomalib expose their supported AMP or precision controls and record
  the effective mode in run metadata.
- Spectrogram XCiT and DETR training adopt the common precision policy when
  their training entry points are implemented.
- Existing callers that omit precision retain a documented compatible default.
- CPU-only execution remains supported with an appropriate full-precision
  setting and a clear error for unsupported mixed-precision requests.
- Tests verify forwarding of 32-true, 16-mixed, and bf16-mixed where supported,
  plus framework-equivalent settings for adapter-based models.
- Checkpoint save/load and inference remain compatible with models trained in
  every supported precision mode.
- Representative GPU benchmarks compare throughput, peak memory, convergence,
  and final validation metrics against 32-true for each model family.
