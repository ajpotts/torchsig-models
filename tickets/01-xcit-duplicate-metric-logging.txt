Title: Prevent duplicate XCiT metric logging

Criticality: High
Estimated effort: Low
Type: Bug

Description:
XCiTClassifier logs train_loss, train_acc, val_loss, and val_acc directly from
its training and validation steps. XCiT training also installs
ClassifierMetricsTrackerCallback, which logs the same metric names at epoch end
with different Lightning logging options.

PyTorch Lightning can reject repeated calls for the same metric name when their
logging metadata differs. Even when accepted by a particular Lightning version,
the two producers make it ambiguous which value is used by checkpointing and
reporting.

Evidence:
- torchsig_models/models/iq_models/xcit/xcit1d.py, training_step and
  validation_step
- torchsig_models/utils/classifier_metrics_tracker.py, _log_metrics
- torchsig_models/models/iq_models/xcit/xcit1d_train.py installs the callback
  while ModelCheckpoint monitors val_loss

Proposed resolution:
Choose one owner for aggregate metrics. Prefer having the callback own epoch
metrics and changing or removing the overlapping logging in XCiTClassifier.
Ensure ModelCheckpoint continues to receive an epoch-level val_loss.

Acceptance criteria:
- XCiT training completes at least one train and validation epoch without a
  duplicate-metric logging error.
- Each public metric name has one unambiguous producer.
- val_loss remains available to ModelCheckpoint at validation epoch end.
- A regression test exercises XCiTClassifier with
  ClassifierMetricsTrackerCallback attached.
